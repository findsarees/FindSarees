Local editing dashboard: Changes are saved only in this browser until you download products.json and upload it to your GitHub repository. This page does not accept payments or update your website automatically. Do not enter passwords or payment credentials here.

Add a product

Upload the image to GitHub in an images folder, then enter its path, e.g. images/saree-01.jpg. You can also use a direct HTTPS image URL.

My products

Import the current products.json from your repository before editing on a different device. Export a backup after changes.

Publish changes to your store

1. Click Download products.json above. 2. Open FindSarees on GitHub. 3. Replace the root products.json with your downloaded file and commit. 4. Wait for GitHub Pages to deploy and refresh your store.

Your checkout is still a demo. Actual Stripe/WooCommerce payment processing and server-side stock checks must be configured separately.
